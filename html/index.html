About the app

One place for every academic responsibility

StudyFlow exists because handwritten notes and messy spreadsheets stop working the moment three assessments are due in the same week. It gives students a structured, single location to organise and track all study tasks.

Each task records a title, the course code and name it belongs to, a due date, a description and any extra notes. Tasks are listed in due-date order with a live countdown, so the next deadline is always obvious — and overdue work is impossible to miss.

The app runs entirely in your browser: tasks are saved locally on your own device, so nothing about your studies is uploaded anywhere.

  • Create tasks

    Record assignments, revision, quizzes and projects with course details and due dates.

  • Update & delete

    Plans change — edit any detail, or remove a task after confirming the deletion.

  • See what's next

    All tasks in one organised table, sorted by due date with a countdown for each.

  • Validated input

    Required fields and past dates are rejected with clear error messages — no broken data.

About me

Hi, I'm Shuhan — and I built this because I needed it.

I'm a first-year IT student taking courses such as INFO1012 Information Technology Systems, INFO1015 Programming Principles and INFO1018 Web & Data Technologies. My academic interests sit mostly with the web: how interfaces are structured, styled and made interactive.

My old study system was a paper diary plus three half-finished spreadsheets — fine in week one, hopeless by mid-semester. Now I plan with a weekly time-block method: every Sunday I list the week's tasks, estimate how long each needs, and block the hardest one into a morning slot. StudyFlow is the digital version of that habit: capture the task when it's announced, watch the countdown, finish it before it turns red.
